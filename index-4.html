About Medela Music Society

Medela Music Society is the musical platform of Ameer Ud Din Medical College, dedicated to nurturing talent, encouraging creative expression, and bringing students together through the power of music. The society gives performers and music lovers a place to take part in memorable events, celebrate artistry, and build a vibrant campus culture.

About the event

This event celebrates talent, creativity, and musical passion. Performers and audiences come together for an evening of rhythm, energy, and entertainment. Register in an open category and become part of an event built around teamwork, performance, and artistic expression. Battle of bands is an intercollegiate event allowing bands of different colleges and universities to compete with each other.
